# Celestial Cycle

## A Design Philosophy for the Chinese Zodiac Calculator

**Space and Form** — The circle serves as the foundational geometry, evoking the cyclical nature of the twelve-year zodiac rotation. Each element orbits a silent center, like constellations rotating around the celestial pole. Negative space is treated with the same weight as filled areas — emptiness becomes a compositional force, allowing the twelve symbols to breathe within their orbital paths. The form language draws from celestial charts and archaic Chinese astronomical diagrams, where precision serves meditative clarity.

**Color and Material** — A restrained palette of vermilion (#C23B22), antique gold (#C9A84C), and deep indigo (#1A1A2E) anchors the visual system. These three colors carry cultural resonance — vermilion as the color of vitality and celebration, gold as the marker of celestial bodies and imperial precision, indigo as the infinite night sky that holds the zodiac in place. No gradient excess, no decorative superfluity. Color fields are applied with the economy of a seal carver.

**Scale and Rhythm** — The twelve zodiac glyphs are arranged in a continuous circular progression, their rhythm established by equal angular spacing and consistent visual weight. Scale relationships follow a deliberate hierarchy: the outer ring of characters sits at a smaller, uniform size to create texture, while the central emblem commands attention through scale and density. This creates a visual pulse — the eye orbits the periphery before settling at the still point of the composition.

**Composition and Balance** — Radial symmetry provides the underlying structure, but subtle variations in character density and stroke complexity create an organic counterpoint to the geometric grid. The composition reads simultaneously as a coherent whole and as twelve individual moments — each character holding its own space while participating in the larger rotation. The center functions as both anchor and void, a necessary stillness around which the cycle turns.

**Expert Craftsmanship** — Every element is placed with the precision of a seal cutter working in stone: each stroke weighted, each gap measured, each alignment refined through countless iterations. The final artifact should convey the hand of a master calligrapher working within the constraints of mathematical precision — as if carved into a jade bi disc that has waited centuries to be unearthed. No element is accidental; no space is unfilled in intent, even when empty in form.

**Typography as Constellation** — Text appears sparingly and only as essential identifier. The app name "生肖计算器" sits with the weight of a temple inscription — few characters, each carrying meaning. Latin text, where present, functions as annotation, rendered in a weight that complements without competing. The typographic voice is that of a museum label: present but deferential, informative but silent.
